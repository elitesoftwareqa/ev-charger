Testing

Four techniques keep the tests honest

Contract tests

One set of tests runs on every charger type, another on SQLite and the stub, so the two cannot drift apart.

Mocks and stubs

A strict gmock fails on any unexpected call: the factory may only read, and storage errors must reach the caller.

Master suites

End-to-end card-tap and app suites (14 and 17 scenarios) run on every charger and check every receipt.

Coverage gate

CI fails below 95% line coverage. Today: 99.5% of lines and 66.8% of branches.

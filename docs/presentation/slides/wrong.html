What happens when things go wrong

SituationResult
Card declined or terminal offlineNo charging; no hold left behind
Charger faulted or occupiedApp returns ChargerUnavailable before any hold
Money runs out firstCharging stops; billed exactly the hold; BudgetReached
Battery fills firstCharger finishes itself; unused hold released; BatteryFull
Phone goes offlineStop fails with NotConnected; the charger keeps going
Terminal drops before capturepaid = false: energy delivered, nothing billed
Charger fault mid-chargeDelivered energy is settled; fault cleared at the end

Key flows

Card-tap flow: five steps, one receipt

1

Hold

The terminal places a hold. If it is declined, nothing else happens.

2

Start

The charger plugs in and starts. Battery energy is recorded as the before reading.

3

Charge

Power is capped by the charger. If the money runs out first, charging stops.

4

Fill

Energy goes into the battery. A full battery finishes the charge by itself.

5

Settle

The real cost is captured, never above the hold. The rest is released.

Receipt: battery energy before and after, energy delivered, amount charged and amount released.
